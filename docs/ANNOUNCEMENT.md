**Title:** Othoughts: mind maps on Canvas, with collapsible branches, auto layout, outline, snapshots and FreeMind export

---

Hi everyone,

I missed iThoughts inside Obsidian, so I built **Othoughts**: a plugin that turns Canvas into a mind-mapping tool. It works on ordinary canvas files, so there is nothing to convert and your notes, images and links stay what they are.

**What it does**

- **Keyboard first:** `Tab` adds a child, `Enter` a sibling, and the new card is ready to type in. Arrow keys move along the map.
- **Whole branches:** collapse and expand any branch from a button on the card; drag a card and its branch comes along; drop it next to another card to re-attach it.
- **Auto layout** to the right, left, down, up or on both sides of the root, with three levels of automatic alignment per canvas.
- **Tidy arrows:** while you drag, arrows switch to the sides that face each other, and smooth curves replace the tight hooks between nearby cards.
- **Styles:** seven map styles and seven backgrounds, chosen independently.
- **Outline view** of the whole map, collapsed branches included, editable.
- **Snapshots:** keep the map as it is at a given moment, by hand or once a day automatically, and browse its history on a timeline. Snapshots open read-only and can be restored.
- **Export** to FreeMind (.mm, which iThoughts, XMind, Freeplane, MindManager and others open), OPML and Markdown.

No network access, no telemetry, no default hotkeys. English and Spanish interface.

**Install:** Settings → Community plugins → Browse → "Othoughts".
**Source and issues:** https://github.com/luisgarciavalina/othoughts

It relies on Canvas internals that are not part of the public API, so a future Obsidian update may need a plugin update. If something breaks, an issue on GitHub helps a lot.

Feedback is very welcome.
